TL;DR: 本文研究任意特征有限域上具有指定伽罗瓦壳维数的广义Roth-Lempel码，提出基于归一化拉格朗日系数与列乘子选择的统一构造方法，给出六种显式构造，并获得MDS/AMDS码及相应的EAQECC、Hermitian LCD码。

Motivation: 伽罗瓦壳维数是构造纠缠辅助量子纠错码（EAQECC）的重要参数，而现有工作在任意特征有限域上对广义Roth-Lempel（GRL）码的伽罗瓦壳维数缺乏系统的构造方法。

Method: 建立基于归一化拉格朗日系数与合适列乘子选择的通用构造框架；从乘法陪集、迹纤维、加性子空间陪集以及混合加性—乘法纤维出发，得到六种显式构造；针对扩张次数 s=2 与 s=3 进行具体设计。

Result: 构造出具有特定伽罗瓦壳维数的MDS与AMDS GRL码；所得码给出具有显式维数、纠缠消耗和较大最小距离的EAQECC；取零维壳时还可得到Hermitian LCD GRL码及相应的最大纠缠EAQECC。

Conclusion: 该统一构造方法在任意特征有限域上实现了对GRL码伽罗瓦壳维数的精确控制，扩展了MDS/AMDS码与EAQECC的构造范围，并统一涵盖LCD码与最大纠缠EAQECC等特例。

Abstract: The dimension of a Galois hull is an important parameter in the construction of entanglement-assisted quantum error-correcting codes. In this paper, we study generalized Roth-Lempel (GRL) codes with prescribed Galois hull dimensions over finite fields of arbitrary characteristic. We develop a common construction method based on normalized Lagrange coefficients and suitable choices of column multipliers, and obtain six explicit constructions from multiplicative cosets, trace fibers, additive-subspace cosets, and mixed additive--multiplicative fibers. For extension sizes $s=2$ and $s=3$, we construct MDS and AMDS GRL codes with Galois hulls of specific dimensions. The resulting codes provide EAQECCs with explicit dimension, entanglement consumption, and relatively large minimum distance. Taking zero-dimensional hulls also gives Hermitian LCD GRL codes and the corresponding maximally entangled EAQECCs.

*Raphael Zailer,Isaac Keslassy*

Main category: cs.NI

TL;DR: CAPS是一种轻量级分布式调度层，通过将速率计算与包调度分离，利用ACK时钟同步发送端相位，在保持高吞吐的同时将队列占用降低5-10倍。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代数据中心传输协议将速率计算与包调度耦合在单一反馈环中，虽能收敛到近优速率，但会产生随流数量增长的standing queues，需要解决这一残余队列问题。

Method: 提出CAPS机制，将速率计算与包调度分离；通过相位锁定将每个发送端的传输与ACK时钟驱动的瓶颈槽同步；针对异构RTT设计每流修正项。

Result: 在dumbbell拓扑下刻画了等RTT、异构RTT和双向流量的锁相稳态；在fat-tree上验证了incast、permutation和all-to-all流量模式；所有场景下最坏队列占用降低5-10倍，且无吞吐损失。

Given stable feasible rates, the residual queue problem reduces to a timing problem: if every flow's packets arrive at the bottleneck in the correct slot, the link stays busy and the queue stays empty. Clocked ACK-Paced Synchronization CAPS is a lightweight distributed scheduling layer that achieves this by phase-locking each sender's transmissions to ACK-clocked bottleneck slots, with a per-flow correction that compensates for heterogeneous RTTs.

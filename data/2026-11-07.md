Motivation: 经典集中问题的特征值在1和0附近成簇，其间由宽度随窗长对数增长、系数为1/π²的plunge分隔。作者希望理解当带不再是单一矩形带、而被变形（平滑边缘、多个不相交带、重叠带、自相似带等）时，这些簇与plunge如何演化。

Method: 以核函数的逐元素幂运算平滑带边；考察互不相交带的副本并用Landau-Widom多区间定理估计plunge展宽；将带收缩为直线得到经典有限秩算子；构造重叠带与自相似（Cantor型）带；对平滑边缘处的重标度特征值进行分析；并考察单带情形下本征滤波器零点在单位圆上的分布。

Result: 逐元素幂平滑带边使plunge转变为幂律边缘，指数比幂次小一；不相交带使plunge宽度按带边数目成比例展宽，系数为逐边常数；收缩带得到有限秩算子；重叠带产生多级阶梯，自相似带产生由Cantor维数支配的簇层级；平滑边缘处重标度特征值不塌缩到单一曲线。边缘描述子依次为常数、指数、整数、分形维数。

Conclusion: 变形带的集中谱由带边缘的几何与正则性刻画：plunge的对数标度系数是逐边常数，带边平滑度决定幂律指数，带的重叠与自相似结构分别导致阶梯与分形层级；单带时主特征向量表现为低通滤波器，随阶数升高更多零点落入带内。

Abstract: A signal band-limited to one rectangular band and observed through a finite window is the classical concentration problem of Slepian, Landau, Pollak, and Widom. The eigenvalues of the operator cluster near one and near zero, separated by a transition called the plunge. The plunge width grows like the logarithm of the window length, with a universal coefficient of one over pi squared set by the band-edge jump. This paper follows the clusters and the plunge as the band is deformed. An elementwise power of the kernel smooths the band edge, and the plunge gives way to a power-law edge with exponent one less than the power. Disjoint copies of the band widen the plunge in proportion to the number of band edges, as the multiple-interval theorem of Landau and Widom implies, so the coefficient is a per-edge constant. Shrinking each band to a line gives the classical finite-rank operator. Overlapping bands give a multilevel staircase, and a self-similar band gives a cluster hierarchy governed by the Cantor dimension. At the smooth edge, rescaled eigenvalues do not collapse onto one curve. The edge descriptor passes from a constant to an exponent to an integer to a fractal dimension. For the single band, classical results place all eigenfilter zeros on the unit circle, with more of them inside the band as the order rises, so the dominant eigenvectors act as lowpass filters.

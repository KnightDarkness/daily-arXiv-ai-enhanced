Motivation: 在OFDM大规模MIMO近场传感中，目标运动会在阵列孔径上引起与天线相关的双基地多普勒变化。忽略这种空间多普勒变化会导致模型失配，从而降低近场定位精度。现有方法要么复杂度低但精度不足（恒多普勒基线方法），要么精度高但计算复杂度过大（4D穷举最大似然估计），缺乏兼顾精度与效率的解决方案。

Method: 提出一个低复杂度递归联合估计框架：首先通过恒多普勒假设进行粗略定位初始化；然后交替执行两个步骤——(1) 基于闭式最小二乘估计器（LSE）的速度估计，(2) 考虑天线依赖多普勒效应的定位精化。通过递归迭代逐步逼近真实目标参数。

Result: 仿真和实测结果均验证了该框架的有效性。与低复杂度恒多普勒基线方法相比，范围、角度和径向速度估计均有提升，并额外实现了横向速度估计。实测中总体定位误差从0.268m降至0.064m，径向和横向速度估计误差分别为0.032m/s和0.069m/s。与高复杂度4D MLE相比，在有限搜索网格的实际条件下，该方法获得可比的测速精度和更精确的定位结果。

Conclusion: 该递归框架在近场传感中有效解决了空间多普勒变化引起的模型失配问题，在保持低计算复杂度的同时实现了高精度联合定位与测速，优于恒多普勒基线方法，并在实际网格约束下优于4D MLE，为大规模MIMO近场传感提供了一种实用的迭代处理方案。

Abstract: In Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM)-based massive Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) near-field (NF) sensing, target motion induces an antenna-dependent bistatic Doppler variation across the array aperture. Ignoring this spatial Doppler variation leads to a model mismatch that degrades NF localization. In this paper, we propose a low-complexity recursive framework for joint radial/transverse velocity estimation and Doppler-aware localization. Initialized by a constant-Doppler coarse localization, the method alternates between closed-form Least Squares Estimator (LSE)-based velocity estimation and antenna-dependent Doppler-aware localization refinement. Simulation and measurement results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework against two benchmark methods. Compared with a low-complexity constant-Doppler baseline method, the proposed algorithm improves range, angle, and radial velocity estimation results, while also enabling transverse velocity estimation. In the measurement results, the overall localization error decreases from 0.268 m to 0.064 m. The radial and transverse velocity estimation errors are 0.032 m/s and 0.069 m/s, respectively. Compared with a high-complexity exhaustive four-dimensional (4D) Maximum Likelihood Estimator (MLE), the proposed method achieves comparable velocity estimation results while yielding a more accurate localization result when the 4D MLE has a practical finite search grid.

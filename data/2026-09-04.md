Method: 采用系统级仿真的方法，构建包含热点嵌入的WCDMA宏蜂窝分层结构模型。设置了两种候选方案（热点区域部署微蜂窝天线、宏基站采用智能天线），并根据不同业务类别用户比例定义了多个仿真场景，通过比较信干比（SIR）相对增益来评估方案性能。

Result: 得到了两种方案在不同业务类别用户比例场景下的SIR相对增益结果，并据此对各场景下最佳方案的选择进行了判定。

Conclusion: 不同业务用户比例下，微蜂窝方案与宏基站智能天线方案的优劣不同，需根据具体场景特征选择最优方案以应对热点流量。

Abstract: The purpose of this contribution is to analyze a hierarchical cell structure with a hot spot embedded in a WCDMA macrocellular system. Particular attention is paid to the impact of introducing smart antennas in this context. A system-level simulation approach is employed, in which we consider two main solutions to address hot-spot traffic: the placement of a microcell antenna or the adoption of smart antenna technology at the macrocell. Relative gains in signal-to-interference ratio (SIR) are then evaluated. Distinct scenarios are defined based on the proportion of users of different service classes. An evaluation of which solution is best suited to each scenario is then conducted.

Motivation: 复杂动力系统的简化对高效估计与控制至关重要，信息论压缩（PF-IB）提供了理论框架，但标准 PF-IB 缺乏结构一致性，所得表示无法作为动力系统实现。

Method: 将 PF-IB 的目标函数约束到一类分块 Hankel 矩阵构成的特定流形上，并证明该流形是一个李群，以此在 PF-IB 与实现理论之间建立联系。

Result: 约束后的压缩动力学保证可实现，可直接用于估计与控制任务；作者通过数值仿真验证了该方法。

Conclusion: 在分块 Hankel 李群流形上施加结构约束，可在保持信息论压缩原理的同时获得可实现、可用于控制的线性动力系统约简。

Abstract: The simplification of complex dynamical systems is essential for efficient estimation and control. A principled approach to this task is through the lens of information-theoretic compression. The Past-Future Information Bottleneck (PF-IB) provides a theoretical framework for compressing linear dynamical systems. However, the standard formulation often lacks structural consistency, yielding representations that are not realizable as dynamical systems. By constraining the objective to a specific manifold of block Hankel matrices, which we prove is a Lie group, we bridge the gap between PF-IB and realization theory. Our approach ensures that the resulting compressed dynamics are realizable, thereby enabling its direct application to estimation and control. We demonstrate our approach in numerical simulations.
